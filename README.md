# Business Sales Performance Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes business sales data to evaluate sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, regions, yearly trends, and the impact of discounts.

The project uses Excel-based data cleaning, Pivot Table analysis, and an interactive dashboard to convert raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify the best-performing regions and categories
- Evaluate customer segment performance
- Analyze yearly sales and profit trends
- Identify profitable and loss-making sub-categories
- Understand the impact of discounts on profitability
- Identify top-performing products
- Develop actionable business recommendations

## 💡 Key Insights

- The West region is the strongest region in terms of profitability.
- Technology is the leading category by sales and profit.
- Consumer is the largest customer segment by sales.
- Overall sales show a positive trend across the analyzed years.
- Tables and Bookcases require attention due to negative profitability.
- Higher discount levels can significantly reduce or negatively affect profit.

## 📌 Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize profitable growth in high-performing regions and categories.
2. Introduce tighter controls on high-discount transactions.
3. Review pricing and cost structures for loss-making sub-categories.
4. Focus promotional efforts on products with strong profitability.
5. Monitor sales, profit margin, regional performance, and discount impact regularly.
